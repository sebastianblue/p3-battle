# Battle of Bands - Shop System Design

## Design Philosophy

This shop system is balanced around the game's **multiplicative scoring formula (TS × SS × AS)** and accounts for:
- SS improvements being 3.6× more valuable than TS improvements
- +1 TS = ~11% score boost, +1 SS = ~20% boost, +1 AS = ~10% boost
- Rainbow cards providing 4.66× tag advantage over Bronze cards
- Specialist (high-tech) strategy being underpowered
- Need for catch-up mechanics and strategic diversity

**Pricing Guidelines:**
- Low impact/utility upgrades: 3-4 money
- Medium impact upgrades: 5-6 money
- High impact upgrades: 7-8 money
- Strategic/conditional upgrades: Variable based on consistency

## PERMANENT UPGRADES (20 Total)

### Economy & Action Economy (4 upgrades)

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 6 money**
**Tour Manager**
Gain +1 Action in the Action Phase.
*You need some help…*

**Balance Note:** Extra actions enable more rehearsals, drafts, and battles. Strong but requires skill to maximize. Priced high due to flexibility.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 3 money**
**Sick Merch**
Gain an extra +2 money at the end of each city.
*Describe your new merch when you get the money.*

**Balance Note:** Generates 8 money over 4 cities. Investment payback after 2 cities. Enables earlier access to other upgrades.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 4 money**
**Side Hustle**
Gain +1 money when you use a Rehearsal token.
*Teaching lessons pays the bills.*

**Balance Note:** Up to 12 money over the game (3 rehearsals × 4 cities). Rewards active play, synergizes with rehearsal-focused builds.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 5 money**
**Discount Connections**
All shop purchases cost 1 less money (minimum 1).
*You know a guy who knows a guy...*

**Balance Note:** Buy early for maximum value. Pays for itself after 5 purchases. Strategic timing decision.

### Roster & Drafting (5 upgrades)

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 4 money**
**Getta Betta Van**
Gain +1 Roster Size (max band size increases from 4 to 5).
*Band needs a van!*

**Balance Note:** Extra member = +3-8 TS, potential style/tag matches, and better audience flexibility. Massively powerful for enabling combos. Limited to 1 purchase.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 5 money**
**A&R Rep**
On your first member draft each round, peek at all four members and pick one.
*Artists + Representation team always diversifies!*

**Balance Note:** Reduces variance, enables strategic planning. Less powerful in late game when needs are specific.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 4 money**
**Talent Scout**
Once per city, you may draft an additional member (don't draw one to replace it).
*They know how to find hidden gems.*

**Balance Note:** Extra draft = more options, better synergies. Depletes draft pool faster. Strong in 2+ player games.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 6 money**
**Genre Specialist**
Choose a style (Rock/Pop/Jazz/Country). Whenever you draft a member with that style, gain +1 money.
*Go all-in on your sound.*

**Balance Note:** Generates 4-8 money if focused. Rewards commitment to City Matcher strategy. Choose wisely!

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 7 money**
**Second Chances**
Once per city, you may swap one band member with one from the discard pile.
*Everyone deserves a comeback tour.*

**Balance Note:** Powerful late-game correction. Access to previously discarded cards. High skill ceiling.

**In-Ear Monitors (and Backing Tracks..!)**

**Balance Note:** Situational but game-deciding in competitive moments. Meta-dependent value.

**Street Team**

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 6 money**
**Arranger on Retainer**
Add +1 SS to 1 rehearsal token used, once per city.
*A versatile arrangement!*

**Balance Note:** +4 SS total = ~80% score boost. Extremely powerful due to SS's 3.6× efficiency. Priced accordingly.

---

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 5 money**
**Sound Engineer**

*Professional production makes all the difference.*

**Balance Note:** Permanent +2 TS = ~22% score boost across all cities. Helps weak Specialist strategy. Fair cost for consistent value.

**PERMANENT UPGRADE - 6 money**
**Signature Sound**
Choose a tag (Vibe/Spotlight/Fame/Songwriting). You get +1 SS for each Special Taste you complete that requires this tag.
*You're known for something specific.*

**Balance Note:** Potentially +2-6 SS over the game. Rewards tag synergy. Combos with tag-focused drafting.

**Dynamic Setlist**

*Flexibility is key to winning crowds.*

**Balance Note:** Enables otherwise impossible audience cards. High skill cap. Can swing crucial battles.

**Producer in the Studio**

## CONSUMABLES (15 Total)

### Scouting & Information

**CONSUMABLE - 4 money**
**Rainbow Scout**
Search the member deck for the first Rainbow card; swap it with one card in your roster.
*Find that star player.*

**Balance Note:** Rainbow cards are 4.66× more powerful than Bronze. Game-changing if timed right. Fair cost for rarity access.

---

**CONSUMABLE - 3 money**
**Genre Scout**
Name a style (Rock/Pop/Jazz/Country). Reveal cards from the member deck until you find one with that style. Draft it or put all cards back in random order.
*Find exactly what you need.*

**Balance Note:** Targeted search for style matching. Helps City Matcher strategy. Lower cost than Rainbow Scout due to less guaranteed power.

---

**CONSUMABLE - 3 money**
**Talent Radar**
Look at the top 5 cards of the member deck. Put them back in any order.
*Plan ahead.*

**Balance Note:** Information + manipulation. Enables next-draft strategy. Multiplayer disruption potential.

---

**CONSUMABLE - 2 money**
**Inside Info**
Look at the top 3 audience cards in the deck.
*Know what's coming.*

**Balance Note:** Cheap information. Plan your battles accordingly. Value depends on timing.

---

**CONSUMABLE - 4 money**
**Instrument Hunter**
Name an instrument (Bass/Guitar/Vocal/Drums/Keys/Sax). Search the member deck for a card with that instrument. Draft it or shuffle back.
*Need that specific sound.*

**Balance Note:** Precision targeting for audience requirements or instrument gaps. Stronger than genre scouting due to audience synergies.

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Suggested Shop Structure
- **Shop Refresh:** Available at start of each city or after certain triggers
- **Shop Size:** 4-6 items visible at once (mix of permanents and consumables)
- **Reroll Option:** Spend 2 money to refresh shop (prevents bad luck)
- **Purchase Limits:** Most permanents can only be bought once; consumables unlimited

### Starting Economy
- Players should start with ~6-8 money (enough for 1-2 early purchases)
- Money generation: ~3-5 per city from base game mechanics
- Total expected money: 20-30 over full game (enables 4-6 significant purchases)

### Dynamic Pricing (Optional)
- Items could cost more for players with higher scores (catch-up mechanic)
- Alternative: Trailing player gets 1 free consumable per city

### Multiplayer Considerations
- Shop could be shared (first come, first serve) or individual
- Shared shop increases competition and value of quick economy
- Individual shop reduces variance, increases strategic planning

## FLAVOR INTEGRATION

Each upgrade has thematic resonance with band management:
- **Roster management:** Van, A&R Rep, Talent Scout
- **Professionalization:** Sound Engineer, Producer, Tour Manager
- **Fan engagement:** Street Team, Sick Merch, Viral Moment
- **Creative process:** Muse, Creative Flow, Studio Session
- **Performance quality:** In-Ear Monitors, Arranger, Dynamic Setlist

The shop represents the infrastructure and support systems that transform a garage band into a professional touring act, while mechanically supporting diverse paths to victory.
